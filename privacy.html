FrameScout Air 隐私政策

最后更新：2026-11-10

1. 适用范围

本政策说明 FrameScout Air 如何处理你使用应用时涉及的信息。开发者联系邮箱为 hudongsheng356@gmail.com。

2. 开发者不收集应用内数据

FrameScout Air 的当前功能在设备本地运行，不要求创建账户，也不会把导入文件、文件内容、图像、笔记、测量或实验结果自动发送给开发者。应用未接入广告、第三方行为分析或跨应用跟踪服务，也不为这些目的读取广告标识符。

“本地处理”并不表示文件完全没有保存在设备中；下文说明应用的本地存储。

3. 本地处理和存储

导入的 FITS 副本、筛选状态、备注和 PencilKit 批注保存在应用本地空间。恢复列表受应用设置控制；删除列表记录只处理应用副本，不删除原始文件。

应用还可能在本地保存语言、外观等偏好，以及处理任务所需的临时文件。这些数据用于你发起的读取、编辑、分析、恢复和导出操作，不用于广告投放或画像。

4. 文件服务、系统备份与外部链接

你通过系统“文件”选择或导出资料时，可能使用 iCloud Drive 或其他文件服务。文件提供商及系统备份可能按你的设置处理相应数据；其处理受对应服务商政策约束，这不表示应用向开发者上传数据。

如果你主动打开应用中提供的外部参考链接，对应网站可能按其政策记录访问。应用内置的科学数据或处理库本身不等同于联网分析服务。

5. 主动联系支持

只有在你主动通过电子邮件联系支持时，开发者才会收到你发送的邮箱地址、消息以及自愿附上的文件或截图。这些信息用于答复问题和处理支持请求，不用于营销或跟踪。请勿发送不必要的敏感内容；你可以先删除文件中的姓名、设备标识或其他敏感元数据。

支持邮件通过你选择的邮件服务发送，并由开发者邮箱服务接收。开发者只在处理请求及必要后续事项所需的期间保留邮件；你可通过下方邮箱请求删除你主动发送的支持资料，法律要求保留的情形除外。

6. 你的选择与删除

你可以不导入个人文件，并使用应用提供的示例功能（如适用）。若应用提供删除功能，可在应用内移除相应资料。请先导出需要保留的内容，再在系统设置中选择“删除 App”来移除应用及其本地数据；仅“卸载 App”可能保留文稿与数据。

你另行导出的文件、云端副本及系统备份需要在对应位置自行管理。开发者没有可供远程取回或删除应用本地资料的服务器副本。

7. 政策更新

如后续版本改变数据处理方式，本政策会相应更新，并调整上方日期。

8. 联系开发者

有关隐私或支持资料删除的问题，请发送邮件至：

hudongsheng356@gmail.com

FrameScout Air Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-10

1. Scope

This policy explains how information is handled when you use FrameScout Air. The developer can be contacted at hudongsheng356@gmail.com.

2. No collection of in-app data by the developer

The app’s current features operate locally on your device. No account is required. Imported files, file contents, images, notes, measurements, and experiment results are not automatically sent to the developer. The app does not integrate advertising, third-party behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking services, and does not read advertising identifiers for those purposes.

Local processing does not mean that nothing is stored on your device. Local storage is described below.

3. Local processing and storage

Imported FITS copies, review states, notes, and PencilKit annotations are stored in the app’s local container. List restoration follows the app setting. Removing a list entry affects the app copy, not the original source file.

The app may also store language/appearance preferences and temporary processing files locally. This information supports the reading, editing, analysis, restoration, and export operations you request; it is not used for advertising or profiling.

4. File services, system backups, and external links

Files selected or exported through the system Files interface may be handled by iCloud Drive or another provider. File providers and system backups may process data according to your settings and their own policies. This is not an upload from the app to the developer.

If you choose to open an external reference link, that website may record access under its own policy. Bundled scientific datasets or processing libraries do not by themselves constitute online analytics services.

5. Voluntary support communications

If you choose to email support, the developer receives the email address, message, and any files or screenshots you voluntarily send. These are used to respond to questions and handle support requests, not for marketing or tracking. Do not send unnecessary sensitive information; remove names, device identifiers, or other sensitive metadata from attachments first.

Support email passes through your chosen mail service and the developer’s mailbox service. The developer retains messages only as needed to handle the request and necessary follow-up. You may request deletion of voluntarily submitted support information using the address below, subject to applicable legal retention obligations.

6. Your choices and deletion

You can avoid importing personal files and use built-in examples where available. Where the app provides deletion controls, use them to remove relevant local items. Export anything you want to keep before choosing Delete App in system settings to remove the app and its local data. Offloading an app may retain documents and data.

Manage exported files, cloud copies, and system backups in their respective locations. The developer does not hold a server copy from which your local app data can be remotely retrieved or deleted.

7. Updates

If future versions change data handling, this policy will be updated and the date above revised.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion of voluntarily submitted support information, contact:

hudongsheng356@gmail.com
